Online Scams Teens Should Avoid

Being online is fun, but scammers are everywhere. Here’s how to protect yourself.

1. Fake Online Stores

Check reviews before buying. If a deal seems too good to be true, it probably is.

2. Social Media Scams

Never click unknown links in DMs or posts. Avoid sharing personal info.

3. Phishing Emails & Messages

Emails asking for passwords, money, or gift cards are usually fake. Always verify the sender.

4. Online Games & Free Gifts

Free gift scams are common. Don’t give account info or passwords for in-game rewards.

5. Keep Accounts Safe

Use strong passwords, enable 2FA, and don’t share login info with anyone.
