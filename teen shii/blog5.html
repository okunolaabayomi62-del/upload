Digital Life: Social Media & Privacy Tips

Being online is fun, but it comes with risks. Protect yourself with these tips.

1. Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords for each account. Consider a password manager.

2. Think Before Posting

Don’t share personal info like home address, phone number, or school publicly.

3. Adjust Privacy Settings

Limit who can see posts and personal info. Review settings regularly.

4. Watch Out for Strangers

Be cautious when chatting with unknown people online. Scammers and predators exist.

5. Digital Detox

Take breaks from social media. It improves focus, mental health, and sleep.
