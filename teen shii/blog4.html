Boost Your Confidence in 30 Days

Confidence is a skill. With small daily actions, you can grow more self-assured.

1. Daily Affirmations

Start each day saying 3 positive things about yourself. Example: “I can handle challenges today.”

2. Set Small Goals

Achieving small goals builds self-belief. Celebrate each win, even tiny ones.

3. Step Out of Comfort Zone

Say hi to someone new, participate in class, or try a new hobby. Confidence grows with action.

4. Body Language Matters

Stand tall, make eye contact, and smile. Your body sends signals to your brain.

5. Reflect on Achievements

At the end of the week, write what went well. Focus on progress, not perfection.
